At a high level our contribution is threefold: we improve the quantitative MCA error bound for polynomial generators, we prove a refinement of the implication from list-decodability to MCA, and we clarify that in general, these two notions are distinct.

We show that, for every finite field $$\mathbb{F}$$, every nonempty seed set $$S\subseteq\mathbb{F}$$, every linear code $$\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathbb{F}^{n}$$ of relative minimum distance $$\delta_{\mathcal{C}}$$ and every degree $$d \geq 1$$, the univariate powers generator $$G_{d}(x) := (1,x,\dots,x^{d})$$ has mutual correlated agreement for $$\mathcal{C}$$ at relative proximity parameter $$\gamma$$ with error $$O(d\max\{1,n\gamma\}/\lvert S\rvert)$$ below the smaller of the unique decoding radius and the generalized Johnson radius $$J_{d+2}(\delta_{\mathcal{C}}) := 1-(1-\delta_{\mathcal{C}})^{1/(d+2)}$$, with a one-coordinate margin below the latter when it is smaller.

Then, we show that list-decodability at radius $$p$$ with list size $$L$$ implies MCA slightly above $$1-\sqrt{1-p}$$ (the improvement on the radius is dependent on the list size). The previous state-of-the-art result of Gao, Cai, Xu and Kan showed that list-decodability at radius $$p<\delta_{\mathcal{C}}$$ with list size $$L$$ implies mutual correlated agreement at every proximity parameter strictly below $$1-\sqrt{1-p}$$.

Finally we construct two families of codes that show obstructions to any improvement of the $$1-\sqrt{1-p}$$ bound, or to strong converse statements. Informally, a bound of the form $$\epsilon_{\mathsf{MCA}}(1-(1-p)^\alpha)\leq Q(n, L)/\lvert\mathbb{F}\rvert$$ for some polynomial $$Q$$ and $$\alpha > 1/2$$ cannot hold for all linear codes $$\mathcal{C}$$ under the sole hypothesis that $$\mathcal{C}$$ is $$(p,L)$$-list-decodable.

Similarly, for any distance $$0<\delta<1$$ and any $$1-\sqrt{1-\delta}<p<\gamma<1$$, there exists a family of linear codes with fixed positive rate and relative distance tending to $$\delta$$ such that $$\lvert\mathbb{F}\rvert\epsilon_{\mathsf{MCA}}(\gamma)\leq \mathrm{poly}(n)$$ but the list size at radius $$p$$ is $$n^{\omega(1)}$$. Both counterexample families exist over fields of any characteristic. Both MCA errors are for the line generator.
